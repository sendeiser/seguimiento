# Product

## Register

product

## Users

**Teachers** — Use the platform during class from a desktop/tablet/projector to record attendance, enter grades in live sessions, manage courses and students, and configure rewards. Primary workflow is speed and accuracy during class time.

**Students** — Access from mobile primarily, at home or during breaks. Check grades, track XP and levels, spend coins in the shop, collect Pokemon, trade with peers, and view the global ranking.

**Tutors/Parents** — Access via public shared links (`/live/:token`, `/class-live/:token`) from mobile. View student progress, grades, attendance, and gamification stats in real time.

## Product Purpose

Notyx Edu is a gamified academic tracking platform that makes grading and progress tracking engaging for students while keeping it efficient and professional for teachers. Success looks like: teachers complete grading faster with less friction, students feel motivated to improve through XP/levels/badges, and parents stay informed with zero extra effort from the teacher.

## Brand Personality

Juvenil, divertido, profesional. The tone is playful but never childish — it treats students as young adults. Gamification is a feature, not a gimmick. The interface should feel alive (animations, feedback, confetti) but never chaotic or overwhelming.

## Anti-references

Mediocrity in any form. No generic SaaS templates, no half-baked UI, no inconsistent spacing, no clashing visual languages. If it looks like little effort was put into the design, it fails.

## Design Principles

1. **Juvenile but respected** — Gamification should feel earned, not infantile. Animations and rewards exist to motivate, not to distract.
2. **Speed is a feature** — Teachers grade during class. Every millisecond of friction is time taken from teaching.
3. **Progress must be felt** — XP gains, level ups, streaks, and badges should have tangible feedback. If it changed, the user should know immediately.
4. **Clarity over density** — Student dashboards are mobile-first. Information should be scannable, not buried in tables or cards.
5. **High contrast by default** — Accessible contrast ratios everywhere, not as an afterthought. Readable in any lighting condition (classroom projector, outdoor mobile).

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA minimum, aiming for AAA where practical
- High contrast as a core requirement, not a mode
- Support for reduced motion (`prefers-reduced-motion`)
- Keyboard navigable throughout (teachers using keyboard-heavy workflows)
